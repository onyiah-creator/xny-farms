About Us

Rooted in Nigerian agriculture

XNY Farms Limited is a Nigerian agricultural enterprise built around a simple idea: get honest, high-quality farm products from source to customer, reliably and at fair value.

About XNY Farms

Company Overview

XNY Farms is a private limited company created to modernize Nigeria’s agricultural sector. XNY Farms Limited operates as a business focused on deploying technical solutions to agricultural needs across domestic and international markets through technology-driven agro-commodities and services. The company holds an export license for agricultural commodities, and its operations span mechanized farming, marketing of harvested produce, secondary/value-added processing, and research and development.

XNY Farms Limited runs a farmhouse alongside multiple ranches and varied farmland, with a goal of building local manufacturing capacity to create jobs, curb brain-drain, and supply goods to both urban and rural communities — with future plans to generate bio-energy from biomass and supply surplus power to the National Grid.

🎯

Our Mission

Our Vision

What Drives Us

Our Goals

  • Run cash and food crop farming, animal husbandry, and agro-allied services safely and legally
  • Bridge the price gap between producers and consumers of agricultural produce
  • Provide extension services and improved, disease-resistant seedlings to smallholder farmers
  • Invest in storage and transport infrastructure to preserve freshness from farm to market
  • Export surplus produce to help stabilize domestic prices
  • Medium-term: expand into mixed farm operations covering crops, livestock, seedling cultivation, manure recycling, biogas energy, an internet-based commodity trading platform, and continued R&D

How We Work

Quality Assurance & Operations

XNY Farms Limited follows kaizen principles: a philosophy of continuous, incremental improvement in quality and waste reduction, applied at every level of the organization. Our commodities are inspected and certified through an in-house quality process spanning planting through harvest, and dedicated plant and animal health specialists inspect our farms and production lines to ensure the highest HSE standards are met.

Beyond Agriculture

Digital Innovation Division

XNY Farms Limited is expanding beyond agriculture into digital technology services as part of its long-term growth strategy. Through this initiative, the company is developing and commercializing WatchVisionAI, an AI-powered entertainment discovery platform, currently under active development.
