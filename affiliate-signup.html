How It Works

Join our affiliate programme

Join our affiliate program and earn 8% commission on every completed sale through your referral link. Fill out the form and we’ll be in touch with your personal referral code.

  1. Apply. Send us your details using the form.
  2. We review your application. Our team reviews every application personally and replies by email.
  3. Get your code and link. If approved, we’ll email you your own referral code and a ready-to-share link.
  4. Share and earn. Every completed sale through your link earns you 8% of the order value.
Commission is 8% of the order subtotal (the value of the products ordered), calculated before any delivery fee is added.
